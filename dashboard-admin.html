Frequently Asked Questions
How do I add a new client? +
Go to the Clients section from the sidebar and click "+ Onboard Client". Fill in the client's details, assign a role, and connect their ad accounts. They'll receive an email invitation to join.
How are reports generated? +
Reports are auto-generated on the 1st of every month for all active clients. You can also manually generate reports any time from the Reports section. Reports include impressions, CTR, spend, and ROAS.
How do I pause or stop a campaign? +
Navigate to Campaigns, select the campaign you want to pause, and use the status toggle. For Google Ads and Meta, changes sync within minutes. YouTube Ads may take up to 1 hour.
What does CTR mean? +
CTR (Click-Through Rate) is the percentage of people who clicked your ad after seeing it. A higher CTR generally means your ad creative and targeting are effective. Industry average is 2–5%.
How do I invite team members? +
Go to Team Access from the sidebar and click "+ Invite Member". Enter their email and assign a role. They'll receive an email with login instructions.
