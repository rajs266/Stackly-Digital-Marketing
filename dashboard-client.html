FAQs
How do I request a new campaign? +
Click "+ Request Campaign" from your dashboard or campaigns page. Fill in your campaign brief — target audience, budget, goal, and timeline. Your account manager will review and set it up within 48 hours.
How do I download my reports? +
Go to the Reports section from the sidebar. Click "⬇ Download" next to the report you need. Reports are available in PDF format and include all key metrics.
Why is my campaign paused? +
Campaigns can be paused if the creative assets are under review, the budget is exhausted, or your account manager has flagged something. Check your notifications or message your account team for details.
How can I upload new creative assets? +
Go to Creative Assets from the sidebar and click "+ Upload Asset". Supported formats: JPG, PNG, SVG, MP4, GIF. Max file size: 20MB. Your account team will review and activate the new creative.
